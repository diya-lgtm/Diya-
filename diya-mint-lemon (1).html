How I'd approach the role

My first 90 days as hypotheses, not promises.

I don't know your internal playbook yet. Here's how I'd think about ramping.

30

Learn the voices

  • Build a voice profile for each client I'm assigned tics, phrases, what they'd never say
  • Reverse-engineer the posts that already worked and why
  • Get the feedback loop tight with editors so revisions shrink fast
60

Find the rhythm

  • Own a client roster end-to-end: notes → drafts → shipped, on cadence
  • Test hook patterns and formats, keep what earns saves and replies
  • Turn recurring client themes into repeatable content pillars
90

Compound it

  • Tighten a personal system so quality holds as volume grows
  • Share what's working so the whole team writes better hooks, not just me
  • Tie output back to what clients actually want: inbound, authority, the right eyes
